Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluefin Tuna LLC handles information in connection with the official bluefintuna.life website and general business communications.

Effective date: March 30, 2026

Overview

Bluefin Tuna LLC operates the bluefintuna.life website as its public company website. We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the site, respond to inquiries, and support legitimate business communication.

Information We May Receive

  • Contact information you provide when you email us.
  • Basic technical information that may be generated by normal website hosting and server logs.
  • Information needed to respond to business, support, or organization verification inquiries.

Website Access And Technical Logs

Like most websites, basic technical data may be generated through web hosting and server operations, such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and related diagnostic information.

This website does not currently provide public user account registration, public comment tools, or public submission forms beyond standard email contact.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to messages, requests, and business communications.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our website and digital services.
  • To support company, product, publishing, or platform-related operations when applicable.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers, hosting providers, professional advisers, or platform operators when reasonably necessary for business operations, legal compliance, security, or verification.

Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Infrastructure

Our website may rely on third-party hosting or technical infrastructure. Those providers may process information under their own terms, privacy notices, and security practices.

Your Choices

If you prefer not to share information with us, please do not send it by email. If you contact Bluefin Tuna LLC, avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact Bluefin Tuna LLC using the methods below.

Bluefin Tuna LLC — Project Portal Privacy Supplement

Version: service-terms-2026-10-30-v2 Effective date: 2026-10-30

1. Scope and contact

This supplement explains how Bluefin Tuna LLC handles information for its planning-service ordering, temporary intake, private workspaces, billing and customer-delivery portal. It supplements, rather than replaces, our existing website and application privacy disclosures. Contact support@bluefintuna.life for portal privacy questions or rights requests.

For information we collect to run customer relationships, billing, security and the portal, Bluefin Tuna LLC determines the relevant purposes and means. If a customer asks us to process personal data solely on its instructions within project documents, the parties must determine their respective roles and any required data-processing terms before transfer. The basic portal agreement is not a promise that every regulated or sensitive-data project is suitable.

2. Information handled

Depending on the order, we handle customer/contact name, business name, email, billing address, tax identifiers where relevant, project reference and location, selected scope and add-ons, quotes, accepted policy versions, payment and invoice references, project documents, deliverables and support communications.

We keep technical records needed for authorization, payment reconciliation, transfer integrity, permitted downloads, deletion and security. These may include timestamps, file types and sizes, cryptographic checksums, internal order/file identifiers, access/revocation status and error records. Necessary network/security metadata may also be processed by infrastructure providers; we do not promise that no IP address is ever processed.

Stripe processes payment-card details. We do not intentionally store full card numbers or card security codes in our portal database. Do not place financial credentials, passwords, government identifiers or unnecessary personal information in project files.

3. Purposes and grounds

We use relevant information to assess and accept orders, determine the agreed scope and applicable billing/tax treatment, collect payment, perform the service, deliver files, provide support, maintain necessary financial/contract records and prevent unauthorized access or fraud.

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis, the appropriate basis may include performing or preparing a contract, legal obligations, or legitimate interests such as securing the portal and dealing with business contacts, subject to the applicable balancing and rights requirements. Where consent is required for a particular optional use, it is requested separately. Accepting the Service Terms does not waive privacy rights or authorize unrelated marketing.

4. Service providers and access

The principal providers in this service are:

  • Stripe: payment processing and related receipt, invoice, fraud and transaction functions.
  • Cloudflare: hosting, access protection, bot verification, temporary R2 file storage and D1 order/operational records.
  • Google Drive: a private project working repository controlled by our authorized operator, used through a connected account to prepare and retain the purchased work.

The initial working repository uses a personally held Google Drive account with access restricted to the authorized operator and the application's permitted files. This is not a publicly shared customer directory or a guarantee of a particular enterprise Google Workspace contract. We do not claim that a specific data-processing agreement or international-transfer mechanism has been executed unless it has actually been put in place.

Customers obtain approved deliverables through the Bluefin Tuna portal. A Google account is not required, and the customer's ordinary portal response does not expose our Drive OAuth credentials, raw Drive identifiers or public Google sharing URLs.

Authorized staff/advisers may receive necessary information for service performance, support, accounting, security or legal matters. Additional document access is limited to a legitimate need. We do not publish project files as marketing examples without separate permission.

6. International processing

Our service is operated internationally. Cloudflare, Google, Stripe, their relevant infrastructure and authorized operators may process information in countries other than the customer's country. Incorporation or payment collection in the United States is not a promise that all processing or storage is exclusively in the United States or in the European Union.

Where applicable law requires particular transfer safeguards, contractual arrangements, location controls or regulated-data protections, those must be addressed before the affected information is accepted. Customers with a strict residency, localization or regulated-data requirement should contact us before upload; the standard portal does not automatically satisfy those requirements.

7. Security and your responsibilities

We use private storage, access controls, limited delivery authorization, verification of relevant payment events, format/size restrictions, transfer-integrity checks and a controlled document-review process. Checksums are used to detect byte mismatches; they do not certify that content is free from malicious material. We make no absolute security guarantee.

Keep your portal invitation, passcode and session secure. Share only information you are authorized to provide. Do not upload unrelated sensitive data or executable/macro content not expressly accepted by the service. If access is lost or compromised, contact support rather than sending secrets in ordinary email.

8. Your rights and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to obtain information about processing, access or correct personal data, request deletion or restriction, object to certain uses, obtain portability, or withdraw consent for processing that actually relies on consent. You may also have a right to complain to a competent authority.

We verify requests proportionately and do not automatically ask for a passport or other excessive identification. Some information must be retained for lawful accounting, contractual, security or dispute reasons; where applicable, we explain the relevant limitation. If a project document contains another person's data, the relevant business customer may need to participate in the response.
