Overview

SnappiQ does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. The app works fully offline and does not require account creation.

What We Collect

We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or files
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data

Tracking & Advertising

No analytics, tracking, profiling, or third-party advertising services are used. No data is transmitted to external servers as part of normal app functionality.

Children’s Privacy

SnappiQ is intended for a young audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or any users.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated in future versions. If future features require data processing, this page will be updated and changes will be clearly disclosed.
