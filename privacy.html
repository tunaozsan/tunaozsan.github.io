Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Bluefin Tuna LLC handles information in connection with the official bluefintuna.life website and general business communications.

Effective date: March 30, 2026

Overview

Bluefin Tuna LLC operates the bluefintuna.life website as its public company website. We aim to collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the site, respond to inquiries, and support legitimate business communication.

Information We May Receive

  • Contact information you provide when you email us.
  • Basic technical information that may be generated by normal website hosting and server logs.
  • Information needed to respond to business, support, or organization verification inquiries.

Website Access And Technical Logs

Like most websites, basic technical data may be generated through web hosting and server operations, such as IP address, browser type, request timestamps, and related diagnostic information.

This website does not currently provide public user account registration, public comment tools, or public submission forms beyond standard email contact.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to messages, requests, and business communications.
  • To maintain, secure, and improve our website and digital services.
  • To support company, product, publishing, or platform-related operations when applicable.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information with service providers, hosting providers, professional advisers, or platform operators when reasonably necessary for business operations, legal compliance, security, or verification.

Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purpose for which it was collected, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Third-Party Infrastructure

Our website may rely on third-party hosting or technical infrastructure. Those providers may process information under their own terms, privacy notices, and security practices.

Your Choices

If you prefer not to share information with us, please do not send it by email. If you contact Bluefin Tuna LLC, avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for your inquiry.
