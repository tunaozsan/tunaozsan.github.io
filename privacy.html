Overview

SnappiQ does not collect, store, or share any personal data from users. The app is designed to work fully offline and does not require account creation.

Data Collection

We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, or any other user data. SnappiQ does not use analytics, tracking, profiling, or third-party advertising services.

Data Sharing

Because SnappiQ does not collect user data, we do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties. No data is transmitted to external servers as part of normal app functionality.

Children’s Privacy

SnappiQ is intended for a young audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or any users.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated in future versions. If we introduce features that require data processing, we will update this page and clearly disclose changes before they take effect.
