Privacy Policy — SnappiQ

SnappiQ is designed with user privacy as a core principle.

SnappiQ does not collect, store, process, or share any personal data from users. The application functions fully offline and does not require account creation, registration, or user identification.

No analytics services, tracking technologies, behavioral monitoring systems, or third-party advertising networks are integrated into the app. No data is transmitted to external servers.

All app functionality operates locally on the user's device.

If future versions introduce features that require data processing, this policy will be updated accordingly and clearly disclosed prior to implementation.
