3. Additional activities, revisions and materials

Additional activity allowance is purchased in blocks of up to 50 activities beyond the main package. An extra 1–50 activities is one block; 51–100 is two. Basic Baseline supports up to two added blocks, and Detailed Baseline and Schedule Update up to four. Larger scopes require a quotation. Audit and Score pilot packages do not accept these automatic add-ons.

The block prices are $100 without resources; $175 for Baseline with labor man-hours and cost allocation; and $150 for Update with existing labor man-hour and cost allocation. Additional in-scope revisions are $99 each, up to three subject to scope approval. Added activities or changed scope can affect turnaround; the adjusted date must be disclosed and agreed rather than silently extending the original commitment.

“With Resources” means labor man-hours and cost allocation based on client-supplied approved information. Physical material resources, equipment-hour models, independent estimates, quantity takeoff, BOQ creation, procurement and budget or unit-rate development are excluded unless separately agreed.

Material add-ons are separately selected for concrete (m³), reinforcement steel (metric tons), formwork (m²), structural steel (metric tons), and piping (meters or metric tons as agreed). Each category costs $249 for Baseline loading or $119 for updating existing assignments for one reporting period. The limit is 50 client-supplied BOQ rows per category, not 50 physical units. Each selected category must fit the approved activity limit of its parent order.

Material add-ons require a compatible With Resources main package. Update material assignments require operator verification of existing mapping before payment. New resource dictionaries or first-time loading are not included in an Update price. Add-ons are not independent services unless expressly quoted against an identified existing project.

Do not send confidential project files through public links or unapproved email attachments.

Remove unnecessary personal identifiers, bank/card information, credentials, health data and unrelated third-party data. Additional confidentiality, processing or security requirements must be agreed before documents are uploaded.

8. Intake, working storage and delivery access

Files first enter private Cloudflare R2 temporary intake. New access to intake files ends 12 hours after the upload session is created, whether paid or unpaid. Scheduled cleanup removes the objects after their expiry; physical removal can occur later than the access cutoff, particularly during incidents. Payment does not restart this clock.

After verified payment, necessary inputs may be copied to our private, access-controlled Google Drive project workspace to perform the service. That working copy is separate from R2 intake and is not erased simply because the 12-hour R2 period ends. We verify transfers and handle failures through retries, review or an authorized re-upload without charging for the same service again.

Approved final files are provided through the Bluefin Tuna customer portal, not a public Drive sharing link. Customers do not need a Google account. Only approved deliverables are exposed; internal working folders and credentials are not shared.

The normal final-delivery availability period is 30 days from publication of the approved final delivery. The related private project workspace and final-file archive are scheduled for deletion 90 days from that same publication time. Publication and expiry are shown in the portal. Downloading or opening an invitation does not automatically restart these periods. A materially revised final release has explicitly stated dates; archival deadlines are not silently reset by minor link reissues.

An unpublished project closed by cancellation or inability to proceed is normally scheduled for project-file deletion 30 days after written closure, unless a shorter lawful period or a documented preservation need applies. These project-file rules are separate from required accounting, agreement, security and dispute records, as explained in the Privacy Notice.

Please download the final files before access expires. A link may be single-use for establishing an authorized session; this does not mean that receipt of a technical download is acceptance of the quality of the work. If access fails during the availability period, contact support for secure assistance. Revocation or expiration does not remove accrued statutory rights.
