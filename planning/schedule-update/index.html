Inclusions and exclusions

Each option delivers an updated XER and PDF. Materials are excluded from ordinary resource updates. Material updates are available only as a separate add-on where initial mapping already exists.

Baseline creation, new resource dictionaries, recovery schedules, expert delay analysis and extension-of-time claims are outside scope. Any revision allowance must be confirmed in writing before work begins.

Delivery rules

4 business days Without Resources; 5 business days With Resources. Timing begins after the full reporting-period data is accepted and scope is confirmed. Conflicting progress records or an unsuitable baseline may require clarification or a separate scope.
