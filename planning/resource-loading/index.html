What “With Resources” means

Client-supplied BOQ and rate responsibilities

Provide item descriptions, quantities, units, BOQ lines and intended activity mapping. Material updates also need actual period quantities. Labor and cost work requires your approved budgets, assignment assumptions and relevant unit rates.

BOQ creation, material takeoffs, measurement verification, independent estimates, unit-rate development and independent cost engineering are not included unless separately quoted.

Category limits and charts

Labor/cost category counts and mapping complexity must be agreed in your written quote before purchase. Update work uses existing labor/cost definitions only. S-curves or histograms are delivered only where expressly included in the agreed package scope and supported by supplied data.

From initial loading to updates

Initial loading establishes the mapping. A later material update changes an existing mapping for one reporting period; it does not include the first loading. New categories or a remapped schedule may require another initial-loading scope.
