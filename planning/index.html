Before we begin

The detail behind
the deliverable.

What do I need to provide?

Baseline work starts with an itemized work list or BOQ, quantities and units, scope, drawings or specifications, project dates and execution constraints. Updates require the current P6 XER, approved baseline, data date and accurate progress information. Each package lists its inputs.

What does “With Resources” include?

Labor man-hours (MH) and cost allocation only, using your approved budgets, assumptions and relevant unit rates. Material resources, equipment-hour models, quantity takeoffs and independent cost estimation are excluded unless separately agreed.

See resource loading scope
Are material quantities included?

No. Each independent material category is a separate add-on: initial loading is $249; an update to existing mapping is $119 per period. Other materials start at those prices. Up to 50 supplied BOQ lines per category, within the schedule’s activity limit. Complex mapping needs a quote.

Can a resource update add the first resource loading?

No. The With Resources update requires an existing labor/cost-loaded XER and uses its existing definitions. Initial Resource & Cost Loading starts at $599 for an existing approved plan with up to 200 activities, subject to review.

When does the turnaround begin?

The stated business-day turnaround starts after all required inputs are complete, accepted and the scope is confirmed. Missing or contradictory information pauses the start; changes, oversized schedules or additional mapping may require a revised written scope.

How do payment and invoicing work?

Start with a service request so we can confirm fit and payment arrangements. Each core service is a separate one-time purchase; an update covers one reporting period. Ask for your business billing requirements before payment. Direct checkout will be available only through verified, individual Stripe-hosted links.

How should I share confidential project files?

Email support@bluefintuna.life to request transfer instructions first. This website has no upload portal. Do not send confidential XER, BOQ, budgets or drawings in an initial enquiry or place them in a public repository.

Does a package guarantee approval or deadline recovery?

No. Deliverables support project planning and decision-making. Employer or authority approval, a particular project outcome, recovery of deadlines, expert delay analysis and extension-of-time claims are outside the standard package commitment.
