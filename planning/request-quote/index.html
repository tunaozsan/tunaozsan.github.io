What to include

Enough context to begin.

  • Your name, email, country and company (optional).
  • The service or SKU, project discipline and estimated activity count.
  • Project dates, key deadline and a short scope summary.
  • Whether a P6 XER, baseline and BOQ or itemized work list are available.
  • For resource work only: whether approved labor MH, cost budgets and relevant unit rates are available.
  • For material add-ons only: categories, units, BOQ line counts and existing mapping status.

Please do not include confidential budgets, full XER files, drawings, passwords, card details or personal identity documents in the initial enquiry.

After your enquiry

Scope first.
Then the details.

01 / Review

We establish the fit.

Your activity count, complexity and available inputs determine whether a standard package or a tailored quote is suitable.

02 / Agree

We define the commitment.

Deliverables, exclusions, revisions, fees and payment arrangements are confirmed in writing. Review the service terms, refund policy and privacy information.

03 / Begin

Complete inputs start the clock.

Request transfer instructions, provide the agreed information and resolve any gaps. Delivery timing begins when inputs are accepted and scope is confirmed.
