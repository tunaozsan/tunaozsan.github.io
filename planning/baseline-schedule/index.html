Inputs & delivery

A baseline is only as clear
as the information behind it.

Client-supplied documents

Provide a complete BOQ or itemized work list, quantities and units, project scope, milestones and contractual dates, relevant drawings/specifications, calendars and execution constraints.

With Resources only: also provide approved labor MH budgets, labor assignment assumptions, cost budgets and allocations, and relevant unit rates. You retain responsibility for budgeting and source data. Without Resources customers do not need to provide MH, cost or material loading data.

Resource category limits

The number of labor and cost categories and their mapping will be confirmed in your written quotation. “With Resources” means labor MH and cost allocation only; material resources are separate add-ons. S-curves or histograms are included only when explicitly stated in the agreed scope.

Revisions and scope changes

Basic packages include one in-scope revision; Detailed packages include two. Changes to the original scope, additional activities, new calendars, resource remapping or new source documents may require a separate quote. A revision does not create a new project baseline scope.

Delivery rules

Basic Without Resources: 5 business days. Basic With Resources: 7. Detailed Without Resources: 10. Detailed With Resources: 12. Each period begins after complete, usable inputs are accepted and scope is confirmed.

Bluefin Tuna LLC does not independently verify measurements, create BOQs, develop rates or guarantee employer approval under these packages.
