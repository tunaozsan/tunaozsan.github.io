Helpful starting points

Before you send a message.

An app is not behaving as expected

Check for an available app update and restart the app. If the issue continues, send the steps that reproduce it and any visible error message. Avoid deleting an app containing local files unless you have checked how to preserve your content.

I have a purchase or billing question

Tell us the app name and describe the issue without including payment-card information. Apple manages App Store purchases and refund requests. Visit Apple’s purchase support page for an Apple purchase. Planning-service enquiries follow a separate written agreement.

I need help with a planning deliverable

Email your project reference, the relevant reporting period, and a short description of the issue. Describe the requested correction before sharing documents; we will agree how to exchange any needed files.

I have a privacy or deletion request

Email support@bluefintuna.life with the app or service name and the information you want to discuss. Do not send identity documents unless specifically requested through an agreed process. Requests are considered under the applicable policy and law; the information needed depends on the request.

How do I send project documents?

Use the private project portal only when its availability is confirmed. Until then, do not submit files. Contact support@bluefintuna.life for assistance; project documents must use the enabled secure portal, not email. Never put confidential files in a public repository.

For app or service support, email support@bluefintuna.life or call +90 543 675 40 31. Do not send project documents by email. The project portal shows its availability separately.
