Proposed cancellation terms

A fair approach
to changes of plan.

How cancellation, work already performed, and delivery concerns should be addressed in a service agreement.

Draft for discussion — not yet effective. These proposed terms are provided for review. Services are currently offered by enquiry, with scope and contractual terms confirmed in a written agreement before work begins. This draft does not replace an existing agreement or limit mandatory legal rights.

Draft prepared October 28, 2026. This proposal concerns planning and consulting services supplied by Bluefin Tuna LLC.

Before work starts

The proposed policy is a full refund of amounts paid for a valid cancellation received before work begins, subject only to any separately agreed, lawfully nonrefundable third-party costs already incurred. Such costs should be disclosed and authorized in advance; this draft does not assume they exist.

After work starts

The proposed approach is a reasonable, proportionate refund for work that has not been performed, after accounting for documented work completed and any properly agreed third-party costs, subject to applicable law. The basis of any deduction should be explained. No blanket “all sales final” rule applies under this proposal.

Missing or incomplete project information

The delivery clock does not start until required inputs are complete, accepted, and scope is confirmed. An information gap may pause the work or require revised scope. It does not automatically void refund or cancellation rights. If the engagement cannot proceed, the parties should agree a fair resolution reflecting work actually performed.

Incomplete or defective delivery

Contact us with the project reference and a description of the issue. We should first review the deliverable against the agreed scope and discuss correction, completion, or another appropriate remedy. This process does not remove any statutory right to a refund or other remedy.

How to request cancellation or raise a concern

  1. Email support@bluefintuna.life with your name, project or invoice reference, and the service concerned.
  2. State whether you want to cancel, report a delivery issue, or request a refund, and briefly explain the circumstances.
  3. We review the agreed scope, stage of work, and relevant records, and discuss the proposed resolution in writing.

Do not email card numbers, passwords, identity documents, or bank credentials. Any approved refund and processing arrangement should be confirmed through the original payment provider where possible. No fixed review or payment-processing time is promised in this draft.

Mandatory rights and existing agreements

Applicable statutory consumer protections take precedence. This proposal does not waive cooling-off rights where they apply, require an invalid waiver, or replace the terms of an existing agreement. Any request to begin services during a statutory cancellation period must be handled lawfully in the final agreement.

App Store purchases

This proposed service policy does not replace Apple’s process for App Store purchases. For an app purchase, visit Apple’s purchase support page. For help using an app, contact Bluefin Tuna LLC support.
