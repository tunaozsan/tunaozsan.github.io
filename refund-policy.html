Bluefin Tuna LLC — Cancellation & Refund Policy

Version: service-terms-2026-10-30-v2 Effective date: 2026-10-30 Contact: support@bluefintuna.life

Scope

This policy applies to our project-planning services and compatible add-ons. It forms part of the accepted Service Terms and order summary. It does not replace Apple’s rules for App Store purchases. Mandatory legal rights take precedence.

Before work starts

You may request cancellation before substantive project work begins. The normal remedy is a full refund. A third-party expense can be deducted only if it was specifically disclosed, authorized in advance, actually incurred and lawfully non-refundable. We do not automatically deduct our Stripe processing fee from your refund.

Successful payment, opening an automated workspace or merely copying your files does not, by itself, mean that all of the professional work has been performed. We keep a record of substantive work commencement and progress.

After work starts

You may request cancellation after work has begun. We assess the completed and remaining work against the accepted scope and provide a reasonable, itemized calculation. A deduction must be supported by documented work or separately authorized third-party costs, must not exceed the relevant agreed fee, and remains subject to mandatory rights. We do not apply a blanket “all sales final” rule.

Missing information or an order we cannot perform

Incomplete inputs may delay the start of work, but do not automatically eliminate cancellation or refund rights. We explain what is missing and offer a practical way to proceed, modify or close the order. If we cannot accept or provide the purchased service, we refund the unperformed portion and provide any additional remedy required by applicable law. If no substantive work has begun and no authorized lawful third-party cost applies, the refund is full.

Incomplete, corrupted or materially nonconforming deliverables

Contact us with the order reference and a description of the problem. We compare the deliverable with the agreed scope and propose correction, completion, re-delivery or an appropriate refund or price reduction. Correcting a material failure to meet the purchased scope is not automatically charged as an additional revision. This review does not waive any statutory remedy.

A scheduling recommendation you decide not to use, or a project outcome outside the agreed deliverable, is assessed against the actual contract rather than treated as an automatic refund entitlement or an automatic exclusion.

File expiry and access problems

The 12-hour temporary intake deadline, 30-day final-download availability and 90-day private archive policy are disclosed separately. These technical time limits do not erase legal rights or evidence of your payment. A paid order with expired or unsuccessful intake may be resolved through authorized re-upload without charging again for the same service.

If you report a download failure during the availability period, we provide reasonable secure assistance. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, invitation secrets or unrelated confidential files in a support message. Deleting a project-file archive does not automatically delete billing or consent evidence.

How to request a refund

Email support@bluefintuna.life with your order or invoice reference, the service concerned and your request. We explain the proposed outcome in writing. Any legally applicable response/refund period prevails. We do not promise an arbitrary universal bank-credit date; once a refund is approved, we initiate it promptly through the original payment method where possible and provide its status or reference. The issuer/payment provider controls the subsequent posting time.

We do not ask you to pay an additional fee to release a refund. A change to a different recipient or bank account requires a secure, documented process and is not made on an unverified email instruction.

Consumer rights and early performance

The standard launch flow is for business/professional orders. If mandatory consumer protections apply to a particular purchase, including withdrawal or cancellation rights, those rights remain applicable. A general terms checkbox is not a substitute for a legally required separate express request for early performance or acknowledgment of the effects of full performance. Such a transaction is reviewed before early work or charging where required.

Payment disputes

We investigate disputes using the accepted scope, communications, actual work and delivery records. A dispute or suspected duplicate payment may temporarily suspend further work or download authorization while reviewed. We do not rewrite historic consent, impose an automatic penalty, or retain more project data than necessary solely because a concern was raised.

For app or service support, email support@bluefintuna.life or call +90 543 675 40 31. Do not send project documents by email. The project portal shows its availability separately.
